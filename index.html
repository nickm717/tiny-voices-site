Why it exists

You have thousands of photos. You have hardly any recordings of how they talk: the little words, the first phrases, the voice that keeps changing while you aren't looking.

Tiny Voices is made for those moments. It's quick enough to catch them while you're holding a toddler, and calm enough to come back to on a slow Sunday.

  • One big bookmark button, and no typing in the moment
  • Shown on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island while it records
  • Clip some bookmarks now and the rest whenever you get to them

Our promise

Your child's voice is not training data.

Some apps in this space run AI on children's recordings, transcribing them, classifying them, even cloning their voices. We decided at the start that Tiny Voices never will. Not on your phone, not in the cloud, not as an option you can switch on.

  • ✳︎No AI on your child's voice, ever
  • ✳︎No transcription, no voice cloning
  • ✳︎No account to create
  • ✳︎No ads, no tracking, no data sold
  • ✳︎Everything lives on your iPhone

Questions

Good to know.

Where are the recordings stored?

On your iPhone. Recording and playback never need the internet. If you turn on iCloud backup, copies go to your own iCloud Drive as plain audio and text files, which only you can get to, and which you can open even without Tiny Voices.

Do you use AI on the recordings?

No, and we never will. Tiny Voices doesn't transcribe, analyse, classify or synthesise your child's voice, on your device or anywhere else. You type the word yourself, which only takes a second.

Can I keep words for more than one child?

Yes. Each child has their own profile, library and colour, and you can switch between them at the top of the app. Whoever's selected tints the whole app, so you always know whose words you're saving.

What's the difference between a quick recording and a session?

A quick recording is for one word or phrase, right now: tap, record, name it. A session is for longer stretches, like bath time or a car ride. It keeps recording while you tap bookmarks, and you clip each bookmark into a word or phrase later.

Is this a speech development or milestone tracker?

No. It's a keepsake. Insights count what you've chosen to save, which is always much less than what your child can say. Typical CDC milestones are shown as gentle reference, never as a score. If you have concerns about your child's speech, talk to your paediatrician.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything works on iPhone alone. The Watch app is a bonus for when your phone is out of reach. It records sessions on your wrist and sends them to your iPhone.

Can I get my recordings out?

Always. Share any clip straight from the app, and the iCloud backup is a folder of ordinary .m4a audio files and text notes, so nothing is locked in.
