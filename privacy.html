Privacy Policy

Last updated October 18, 2026

Tiny Voices exists to keep recordings of your child safe for years, so we built it to collect as little as possible. In short: your recordings and notes stay on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We don't receive them, and we never run AI on them.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you create in Tiny Voices stays on your iPhone: recordings, the words and phrases you type, notes, your children's names, birthdates, photos and colours. Recording and playback work without an internet connection. We have no servers that receive this content, and no account is needed to use the app.

iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup, Tiny Voices copies your recordings and notes to the Tiny Voices folder in your iCloud Drive, as ordinary audio and text files. Apple stores those files under your Apple Account, as covered by Apple's privacy policy. We can't access them. You can turn backup off, or delete the files in the Files app, whenever you like.

Apple Watch

Sessions recorded on Apple Watch go straight from your Watch to your paired iPhone. Once your iPhone has a session, it's removed from the Watch.

No AI, ever

Tiny Voices doesn't transcribe, analyse, classify, clone or synthesise any recording, on your device or anywhere else. We don't use your recordings or data to train any model.

Microphone and camera

Tiny Voices asks for microphone access, and camera access for video, only so you can record. Recording happens only when you start it, and iOS shows its recording indicator the whole time. Session recordings can pick up other people nearby, so we suggest deleting a session's full recording once you've clipped what you want. The app will remind you.

Analytics, ads and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no tracking SDKs, and it does not track you across other apps or websites. If you choose to share crash reports with developers through Apple, Apple gives us anonymous diagnostic data that doesn't include your recordings.

This website

If you join the launch waitlist, we store your email address, use it only to tell you when Tiny Voices is available, and delete it on request. A third-party form service may process the address on our behalf. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs, and the site loads fonts from Google Fonts. The site sets no advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers which colour you picked in your own browser, and that never leaves your device.

Children

Tiny Voices is made for parents and caregivers, not for children to use themselves. Recordings of children stay on the parent's devices and iCloud account, as described above. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Delete any word, phrase or recording in the app. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your iPhone. iCloud backup files stay in your iCloud Drive until you delete them there.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Questions? Email us.
